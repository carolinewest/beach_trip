Smart Planning Guide

Best Timing for Lower Rates & Warm Swimming

To secure significantly lower rental prices while ensuring ocean water is wonderfully warm for swimming (low-to-mid 80s), target these two prime windows:

Option 1: Early-to-Mid June

Right before peak summer school-out spikes in late June/July. Weather is gorgeous, ocean is warming up nicely, and rental rates are 20-30% lower than peak July.

Option 2: Post-Labor Day to Mid-September

The ocean remains at its absolute warmest summer temperatures after months of sun, crowds disappear, beaches are wide open, and rental rates drop drastically.

🌾 Safe Gluten-Free Dining Guide (Wrightsville / Coastal Area)

1. Ceviche’s

Coastal Pan-Latin / Tapas

GF Safety: Outstanding. Naturally GF options, separate fryers, very accommodating. Perfect for Mom's tapas!

Distance: ~6 min walk from beach houses.
Reviews: 4.8/5 for vibrant atmosphere & sangria.

2. Tower 7 Dalee Mexican Grill

Baja-style Mexican Tacos

GF Safety: Corn tortillas, chips, and dedicated GF modifications available. Kid-friendly favorite.

Distance: ~4 min walk.
Reviews: 4.7/5 for quick service & casual vibe.

3. South Beach Grill

Upscale Seafood & Coastal Plates

GF Safety: Knowledgeable servers, customizable fresh catch and grilled seafood.

Distance: ~6 min walk.
Reviews: 4.9/5 for trendy dining & cocktails.
